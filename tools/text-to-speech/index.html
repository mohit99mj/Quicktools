    How to use

    1. Type or paste the text you want to hear.
    2. Pick a voice from the list and adjust the rate and pitch sliders.
    3. Press "Speak" to listen. Pause, resume or stop the playback anytime.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which voices are available?

    The tool uses the voices built into your browser and operating system, so the list varies by device — a phone, a Windows PC and a Mac each offer different voices.

    Does my text get sent to a server?

    No. Speech is synthesized locally by your browser using its built-in engine; your text never leaves the device.

    Why does it sound robotic?

    Built-in browser voices are simpler than cloud AI voices. Quality and naturalness depend on your operating system’s speech engine.

    Can I download the audio?

    Not with this tool — it plays speech through your speakers in real time and does not record audio.
