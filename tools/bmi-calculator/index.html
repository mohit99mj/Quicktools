BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a quick screening measure that relates your weight to your height. Enter both and this free BMI calculator instantly shows your index to one decimal place plus your category: underweight, normal weight, overweight or obesity.

BMI is a useful starting point, but it does not measure body fat directly — athletes with high muscle mass can score high without excess fat. Use it alongside other health markers and talk to a professional about what it means for you.

Your inputs never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Enter your height in centimetres.
  2. Enter your weight in kilograms.
  3. Press Calculate BMI to see your index and category.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI range?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is classified as normal weight. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30 or above is obesity.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI equals weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. For example, 70 kg at 175 cm gives 70 / (1.75 x 1.75) = 22.9.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

Not always. It can misclassify very muscular people, and it may be less meaningful for children, older adults and pregnant women. Treat it as a screening tool, not a diagnosis.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser and nothing leaves your device.
