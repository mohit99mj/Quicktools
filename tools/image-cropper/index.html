How to use

  1. Upload an image (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  2. Drag inside the selection to move it; drag a corner handle to resize it.
  3. Optionally pick an aspect-ratio preset to lock the shape.
  4. Click "Crop & download" to save the cropped image.

Frequently asked questions

Will cropping reduce image quality?

No. Cropping cuts pixels at full resolution — nothing is re-scaled or re-compressed beyond the normal encoding of the downloaded file.

How do I keep a specific aspect ratio?

Pick a preset (1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9) above the image. Resizing the corners then keeps that shape locked. Choose "Free" to crop any shape.

What is the maximum image size?

The editor works with images up to 4000 px on the longest side, but the crop is taken from your full-resolution upload whenever possible.

Can I crop a circle?

Not yet — this tool crops rectangles only. For round avatars, crop a square (1:1) and most platforms display it as a circle.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The image stays in your browser from upload to download.
