Weight Converter

Convert weight and mass between metric units (milligrams to metric tons) and imperial units (ounces, pounds, stones). Results update live as you type, with the exact rate shown.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Type the weight value you want to convert.
  2. Choose the unit to convert from and the unit to convert to.
  3. Read the live result and the exact conversion formula below it.
  4. Use the swap button to flip the conversion direction.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert kg to lb?

Select kg as the "from" unit and lb as the "to" unit. One kilogram equals 2.20462 pounds.

How many ounces are in a pound?

There are exactly 16 ounces in one avoirdupois pound (1 oz = 28.3495 g).

What is a stone in kg?

One stone equals 6.35029 kg (14 pounds). Stones are still commonly used for body weight in the UK and Ireland.

Is this weight converter accurate?

Yes. It uses internationally defined conversion factors (for example, 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg exactly) and shows results to 6 significant digits.
