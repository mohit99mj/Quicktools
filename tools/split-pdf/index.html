Split PDF

Only need a few pages? Split PDF extracts exactly the pages you want into a new PDF — pull out a chapter, a single invoice, or any custom selection of pages.

Type page ranges like 1-3, 5, 8-9 (pages are numbered from 1), preview your selection, and download the new file. The original PDF is never changed.

Drag & drop your PDF here

One PDF file at a time.

Page numbers start at 1. Extracted in ascending order, duplicates removed.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Drop your PDF into the upload area, or click Browse files to select it.
  2. Enter the pages to extract, e.g. 1-3, 5, 8-9. Pages are numbered from 1.
  3. Check the preview of your selection for mistakes.
  4. Click Extract pages and save the downloaded file.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a page range?

Use numbers and dashes separated by commas, for example "1-3, 5, 8-9". Single pages and ranges can be mixed freely. Pages are numbered from 1.

What order will the extracted pages be in?

Pages are always extracted in ascending numerical order, with duplicates removed — even if you type them in a different order.

Does splitting change my original PDF?

No. Your original file is never modified; the extracted pages are saved as a separate new PDF.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your files never leave your device — the entire split runs locally in your browser.
