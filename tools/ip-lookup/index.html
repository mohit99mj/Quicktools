Frequently asked questions

What is my IP address?

It is the public address your internet connection presents to websites — shown in large text above. Copy it whenever a support page or service asks for your IP.

How accurate is the location shown?

Location is approximate: it usually resolves to your city or region based on your ISP’s routing, not your exact address. VPNs and mobile networks can place it far from where you actually are.

Why does location lookup sometimes fail?

The free lookup service is rate-limited. If it is busy, the tool still shows your IP address and you can reload later for location details.

Is my IP address stored by this site?

No. The lookup runs in your browser through third-party APIs, and this site stores nothing about your visit.
