Calorie Calculator

How many calories do you need per day? This free calorie calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the most widely used formula for estimating Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) — then multiplies by your activity level to give your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).

It also shows guideline targets: roughly TDEE minus 500 kcal per day for steady fat loss (about 0.5 kg per week) and TDEE plus 500 for gaining. These are estimates, not medical advice — individual needs vary.

How to use

  1. Select your sex, then enter your age, weight and height.
  2. Choose the activity level closest to your typical week.
  3. Press Calculate calories to see your BMR, TDEE and guideline targets.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is what your body burns at complete rest. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) adds the calories burned through activity and exercise on top of BMR.

Which formula does this calculator use?

The Mifflin-St Jeor equation: for men, 10 x weight(kg) + 6.25 x height(cm) - 5 x age + 5; for women the same with -161 instead of +5. Activity multipliers range from 1.2 (sedentary) to 1.9 (very active).

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

A common guideline is about 500 kcal below your TDEE per day, which typically leads to around 0.5 kg of fat loss per week. Do not go below safe minimums without professional guidance.

How accurate is this estimate?

It is a population-average estimate and can be off by 10% or more for any individual. Track your weight trend over a few weeks and adjust based on real results.
