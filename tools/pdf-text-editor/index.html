PDF Text Editor

PDF Text Editor lets you click on text inside a PDF and replace it in place — fix a typo, update a name or change a date without rebuilding the document.

Each page is rendered in your browser; click any selectable text, type the replacement, and download a new PDF with your edits applied. Edited text is redrawn with Helvetica — original fonts can't be preserved.

How to use

  1. Drop your PDF into the upload area, or click Browse files to choose one.
  2. Wait for the page to render, then click any text on the page to open the editor.
  3. Type your replacement in the box and click Save — edited text is highlighted in green.
  4. Move between pages with the page selector, then click Download edited PDF.

Frequently asked questions

What can I edit with the PDF Text Editor?

You can click on any selectable text in your PDF and replace it in place. The tool renders each page and lets you change the wording while keeping the text in its original position.

Will the edited text keep the original font?

No. Edited text is redrawn with the Helvetica typeface, so the original fonts can't be preserved. The replacement is matched to the original size and position as closely as possible.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

No. Scanned pages are images without selectable text, so there is nothing to click and edit. The tool will tell you when a page has no selectable text.

Can I edit images, tables or the layout?

No. This tool only replaces text strings. Images, tables, drawings and the page layout are left untouched.

Can I edit multiple pages?

Yes. Use the page selector or the previous/next buttons to move between pages. All your edits are saved into one downloaded PDF.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF never leaves your device — rendering and editing happen entirely in your browser.

Why does my replacement text look slightly different?

Because the replacement uses Helvetica with standard spacing, character widths and line breaks can differ slightly from the original font. Keep replacements close to the original length for the cleanest result.
