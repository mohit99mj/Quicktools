Salary Calculator

Turn an annual salary into the pay figures you actually think in. This free salary calculator converts annual gross pay into monthly, biweekly, weekly and hourly rates instantly.

All figures are pre-tax estimates: they do not subtract income tax, social charges, retirement contributions or benefits, which vary widely by country and employer. Your input never leaves your browser.

Pre-tax estimates — actual take-home pay is lower after taxes and deductions.

Your inputs never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Enter your annual gross salary (before tax).
  2. Press Calculate pay to see the monthly, biweekly, weekly and hourly breakdown.
  3. Remember the figures are pre-tax — actual take-home pay will be lower.

Frequently asked questions

Are these figures before or after tax?

Before tax. The calculator shows gross pay only; income tax, social charges and deductions are not subtracted because they differ by location and personal situation.

How is the hourly rate calculated?

Annual salary divided by 2,080 — the standard figure for 40 hours per week over 52 weeks.

What is the difference between biweekly and twice-monthly pay?

Biweekly means every two weeks (26 paychecks a year); twice-monthly means two fixed dates per month (24 paychecks). This calculator uses biweekly (26).

Can I convert an hourly wage to an annual salary instead?

Yes — multiply your hourly wage by 2,080 (40 hours x 52 weeks), then enter that annual figure here to see the other breakdowns.
