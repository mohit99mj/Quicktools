EMI Calculator

Planning a home, car or personal loan? This free EMI calculator shows your Equated Monthly Instalment — the fixed amount you pay every month — along with the total interest you will pay over the life of the loan.

It also builds a full month-by-month amortization schedule so you can see exactly how each payment splits into principal and interest. All calculations run in your browser; nothing is stored or shared.

How to use

  1. Enter the loan amount (principal).
  2. Enter the annual interest rate and the loan tenure in years.
  3. Press Calculate EMI to see your monthly payment, totals and the full schedule.

Frequently asked questions

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12 / 100) and n is the number of months.

What is an amortization schedule?

It is a table showing every monthly payment split into principal and interest, plus the remaining balance. Early payments are mostly interest; later ones are mostly principal.

Does a longer tenure always mean lower EMI?

Yes, stretching the same loan over more months lowers each EMI — but you pay significantly more total interest. Compare both before choosing.

Does this include processing fees or taxes?

No. This calculator shows principal and interest only; lender fees, insurance and taxes are not included.
