UUID Generator

A UUID generator that creates version-4 universally unique identifiers in bulk — up to 100 at a time — using your browser’s cryptographic random number generator. Perfect for database keys, tokens and test data.

Format the output as uppercase, compact no-hyphens, or wrapped in braces to match the style your system expects, then copy the whole list with one click.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

Formatting

How to use

  1. Set how many UUIDs you need (1–100).
  2. Pick formatting options: uppercase, no-hyphens, or braces.
  3. Click "Generate UUIDs".
  4. Copy them all to the clipboard with one click.

Frequently asked questions

What is a UUID?

A universally unique identifier — a 128-bit value written as 32 hexadecimal digits, commonly used as database keys or unique tokens where collisions must be practically impossible.

Are these UUIDs really unique?

Each UUID v4 is generated from 122 bits of cryptographic randomness, making a duplicate astronomically unlikely. They are not centrally registered, so uniqueness is statistical rather than guaranteed.

What do the formatting options do?

Uppercase renders the A–F digits in capitals, no-hyphens gives the compact 32-character form, and braces wraps each value in { } as some Microsoft tools expect.

Is my list stored anywhere?

No. Generation and display happen locally in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.
