Frequently asked questions

How much can image compression reduce file size?

It depends on the image and settings. Photos saved as JPEG or WebP at 70–80% quality are typically 60–90% smaller than the original with little visible difference.

Does compression reduce image dimensions?

No, unless your image is larger than 4000 px on its longest side — then it is scaled down to that cap so your browser stays responsive. Dimensions are shown before and after.

Which format gives the smallest file?

WebP usually wins for photos. JPEG is the most compatible choice, and PNG keeps perfect quality but stays larger — it is best for graphics with sharp edges.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Why does PNG ignore the quality slider?

PNG is a lossless format, so there is no quality tradeoff to adjust. The slider applies to JPEG and WebP only.
