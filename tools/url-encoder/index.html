URL Encoder

Percent-encode URLs for safe use in links, query strings and APIs — or decode encoded URLs back to readable text. Choose "Full URL" to keep the URL structure intact, or "Component" to encode a single value such as a query parameter.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Paste the URL or text into the input box.
  2. Pick Full URL (keeps : / ? #) or Component (encodes everything) mode.
  3. Click Encode or Decode.
  4. Copy the result with one click.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Full URL and Component mode?

Full URL mode uses encodeURI, which leaves structural characters like : / ? # intact. Component mode uses encodeURIComponent, which also encodes those — use it for a single query-string value that may itself contain & or =.

Which characters get percent-encoded?

Spaces become %20, and reserved or non-ASCII characters become %XX sequences based on their UTF-8 bytes. Unreserved characters (A–Z, a–z, 0–9, - _ . ~) are never encoded.

Why did decoding fail with "URI malformed"?

The input contains a % not followed by two hex digits, or a lone % at the end. Check for stray percent signs and try again.

Is my URL sent to a server?

No. Encoding and decoding run locally in your browser — your URLs never leave your device.
