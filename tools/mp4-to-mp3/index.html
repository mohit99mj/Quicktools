MP4 to MP3

Need just the audio from a video? This free MP4 to MP3 converter extracts the soundtrack from any video file and saves it as an MP3, right in your browser. Choose between 128k, 192k and 320k bitrates depending on how much quality you need.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Processing happens 100% in your browser — your files are never uploaded. The engine (~25MB) downloads once. Large files may be slow on phones.

Download MP3

How to use

  1. Choose a video file (MP4, WebM, MOV and more) from your device.
  2. Click "Load audio engine" — a one-time ~25MB download that enables conversion.
  3. Pick a bitrate (128k, 192k or 320k) and press "Convert to MP3".
  4. Play the audio preview and download your MP3.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Extraction happens entirely on your device using an audio engine that runs in your browser. Your file is never sent to a server.

Which bitrate should I choose?

128k is fine for voice and small files, 192k is a good all-round choice, and 320k gives the best quality for music at the cost of a larger file.

What video formats are supported?

Any video format the engine can read — MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, AVI and more. The output is always a standard MP3 file.

What if my video has no audio track?

The conversion will fail with a clear message. The tool can only extract audio if the video actually contains an audio stream.

Why does the engine need to download ~25MB first?

Audio extraction needs a full media codec, which downloads once and is then cached by your browser. After that, conversion runs fully offline.
