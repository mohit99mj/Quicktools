Data Storage Converter

Frequently asked questions

How many MB are in a GB?

It depends on the unit system: 1 GB = 1,000 MB in decimal (SI) units, but 1 GiB = 1,024 MiB in binary (IEC) units. This converter handles both.

What is the difference between MB and MiB?

MB (megabyte) is 1,000,000 bytes (10^6), while MiB (mebibyte) is 1,048,576 bytes (2^20). Storage manufacturers advertise decimal GB; Windows reports binary GiB, which is why a "1 TB" drive shows as ~931 GiB.

Why does my hard drive show less space than advertised?

Drive makers use decimal gigabytes (1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes) while most operating systems display binary gibibytes (1 GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes), so the number looks smaller even though the bytes are the same.
