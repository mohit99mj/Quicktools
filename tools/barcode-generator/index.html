Barcode Generator

A barcode generator that draws CODE128, CODE39, EAN-13, UPC-A, ITF-14, MSI, Pharmacode and Codabar barcodes right in your browser. Pick a format, enter your value, and preview the barcode instantly.

Download the result as a scalable SVG for print work or as a PNG for documents and labels. No fonts or plugins needed — the barcode is drawn as vector graphics on the page.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

CODE128 accepts standard keyboard characters.

How to use

  1. Choose a barcode format from the list (CODE128 is the most flexible general choice).
  2. Enter the value, following the format hint shown under the input.
  3. Click "Generate barcode" to preview it.
  4. Download as SVG for print or PNG for documents.

Frequently asked questions

Which barcode format should I choose?

CODE128 is the most flexible for general use. EAN-13 is standard for retail products worldwide, UPC-A for North America, and ITF-14 for cartons and outer cases.

Why does EAN-13 ask for 12 digits?

The 13th digit is a check digit calculated from the other 12. Enter the first 12 digits and the check digit is added automatically — or enter all 13 if you already have the full code.

Can I print these barcodes?

Yes. Download the SVG version for print work since vector graphics stay sharp at any size, or use the PNG for documents and on-screen labels.

Do I need a barcode font installed?

No. The barcode is drawn as vector graphics directly on the page, so it displays and prints correctly without any special fonts.
