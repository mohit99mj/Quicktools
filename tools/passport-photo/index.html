Passport Photo Maker

Turn any recent headshot into a correctly sized passport or ID photo. Pick the official size for your document, frame your face with the zoom slider, and download a photo at the exact required pixels.

Tip: official rules vary by country — most require a plain light background, a neutral expression and no headwear. This tool sizes the photo; always check your issuing authority's photo rules before submitting.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

JPG or PNG. Use a clear, front-facing photo with a plain background.

Drag the preview to reposition your face. The preview shows the exact final pixel size.

How to use

  1. Choose your ID size: 35×45mm, 2×2in or 50×50mm.
  2. Upload a clear front-facing photo with a plain background.
  3. Drag to reposition and use the zoom slider to frame your head and shoulders.
  4. Click "Download photo" to save the exact-size image.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes does this passport photo maker support?

Three presets: 35×45mm (413×531 px @ 300 dpi, used by India, the EU and many others), 2×2in (600×600 px, US passport) and 50×50mm (591×591 px).

Will my photo be accepted for a real passport?

The tool produces the exact required pixel size, but acceptance also depends on official photo rules — plain background, neutral expression, face size ratios and recency. Check your issuing authority's guidelines.

What kind of photo works best?

A recent, sharp, front-facing headshot taken in even light against a plain white or light background, with your face clearly visible and centered.

Can I print the photo at the right size?

Yes. The download is rendered at the preset's pixel size at 300 dpi, so printing software set to "actual size" produces the correct physical dimensions.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photo is processed in your browser and never leaves your device.
