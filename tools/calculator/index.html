Calculator

A fast, free online calculator that handles full expressions — not just one operation at a time. Type 2*(3+4)^2 or tap the keypad; it follows standard order of operations with parentheses, powers and scientific functions.

Everything is computed instantly on your device. There is no sign-up, no history stored and no network request — your calculations stay private.

0

% is percent: 50% = 0.5, so 200*50% = 100. Trig uses degrees. Keys show ÷ and × but enter / and *.

Your inputs never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Type an expression with your keyboard or tap the keypad buttons.
  2. Use parentheses for grouping, ^ for powers, and % for percent (50% = 0.5).
  3. Open the scientific panel for sqrt, sin, cos, tan, log, ln, pi and e.
  4. Press = or Enter to lock in the result; keep typing to continue calculating.

Frequently asked questions

What does the % button do?

It works as a percent operator: 50% becomes 0.5. So 200*50% equals 100, and 50+10% equals 50.1.

Are the trig functions in degrees or radians?

Degrees. sin(30) equals 0.5, cos(60) equals 0.5, and tan(45) equals 1.

What is the difference between log and ln?

log() is the base-10 logarithm (log(100) = 2) and ln() is the natural logarithm (ln(e) = 1).

Why does the calculator show "Invalid expression"?

The expression has a typo, unbalanced parentheses, an unknown word, or an impossible operation such as dividing by zero or taking the square root of a negative number.

Is my calculation history saved anywhere?

No. All evaluation happens in your browser and nothing is sent to a server or stored.
