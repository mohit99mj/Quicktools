Text to PDF

Convert text to PDF in seconds: paste your text or upload a .txt file, give the document a title, and download a clean, printable A4 PDF set in Helvetica with automatic word wrapping.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser. No signup, no watermark, no limits.

How to use

  1. Paste your text below, or upload a .txt file to load it.
  2. Enter a document title (used as the PDF heading and file name).
  3. Click "Generate PDF" to build the document.
  4. Download the PDF and save or print it.

Frequently asked questions

Is this text-to-PDF converter free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up. The PDF is generated on your device by the pdf-lib library.

Can I convert a .txt file to PDF?

Yes. Use the file picker to load a .txt file — its contents fill the text box, then generate the PDF as usual.

What formatting does the PDF have?

A title heading, body text in Helvetica at 11pt on A4 pages, with automatic word wrapping and page breaks. Blank lines between paragraphs are preserved.

Does it keep rich formatting like bold or tables?

No — this tool works with plain text only. Styling such as bold, italics or tables is not preserved.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is assembled entirely in your browser, so your text never leaves your device.
