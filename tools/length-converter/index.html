Length Converter

Convert between metric and imperial length units in one click. Type a value, pick the units, and the result updates live — from millimeters to miles.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Type the value you want to convert.
  2. Choose the unit to convert from and the unit to convert to.
  3. Read the live result and the exact conversion formula below it.
  4. Use the swap button to flip the conversion direction.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert cm to inches?

Select cm as the "from" unit and in as the "to" unit, then type your value. One centimeter equals exactly 0.393701 inches (1 inch = 2.54 cm).

How many feet are in a meter?

One meter equals 3.28084 feet. The converter shows the exact rate as "1 m = 3.28084 ft" under the result.

What is the formula for miles to kilometers?

Multiply miles by 1.60934 to get kilometers. For example, 10 miles = 16.0934 km.

Is this length converter free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up, and every calculation happens locally in your browser.
