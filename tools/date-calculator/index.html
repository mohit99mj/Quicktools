Date Calculator

Need to know how many days until a deadline, or what date falls 90 days from now? This free date calculator does both: it measures the exact span between two dates (days, weeks, approximate months and years, plus a weekday count) and it can add or subtract days, weeks, months or years from any date.

Month arithmetic clamps to the end of shorter months (31 January plus one month gives 28/29 February), and everything is computed in your browser with no data stored.

How to use

  1. For a date difference: pick the start and end dates, then press Calculate difference.
  2. For date arithmetic: pick a start date, enter an amount, choose the unit and Add or Subtract.
  3. Read the result — spans show weekdays too; arithmetic shows the resulting date.

Frequently asked questions

Does the day count include both the start and end dates?

The day count is the number of 24-hour steps between the two dates (end minus start), while the weekday count includes both endpoints.

What happens when I add one month to 31 January?

The result clamps to the last day of the target month — 28 February in a common year, 29 February in a leap year.

Can I subtract dates as well as add?

Yes. Choose Subtract with a positive amount, or simply enter a negative amount with Add.

Are time zones an issue?

No. Dates are handled as plain calendar dates in UTC, so daylight-saving changes cannot shift a result by a day.
