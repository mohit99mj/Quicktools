Word Counter

A word counter that analyzes your text live as you type. See word count, character count with and without spaces, sentence and paragraph counts, plus estimated reading and speaking time.

It doubles as a character counter for posts, bios and forms with strict limits — the "characters (no spaces)" figure is the one most platforms care about.

Frequently asked questions

Is this also a character counter?

Yes. It shows total characters and characters excluding spaces, updating live as you type — so it works as a character counter too.

How are reading and speaking times calculated?

Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute and speaking time about 150 words per minute. Both are rough estimates; actual speed varies by reader and text difficulty.

How are sentences counted?

Text is split at sentence-ending punctuation (. ! ? …). Abbreviations like "e.g." can inflate the count slightly, so treat it as approximate.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Counting happens entirely in your browser; your text never leaves the page.
