DNS Lookup

Run a DNS lookup on any domain: enter a domain name, pick a record type, and see the live answer returned by Google’s public DNS-over-HTTPS resolver.

Handy for checking A and AAAA records when moving hosts, verifying MX records for email setup, or inspecting TXT records such as SPF and DKIM.

How to use

  1. Enter a domain name, e.g. example.com (no https:// or slashes).
  2. Choose the record type: A, AAAA, MX, TXT, NS, or CNAME.
  3. Click "Look up" to query the DNS.
  4. Read the answer table — TTL and record data are shown for each result.

Frequently asked questions

What DNS records can I look up?

A (IPv4 address), AAAA (IPv6 address), MX (mail servers), TXT (text records like SPF/DKIM), NS (name servers), and CNAME (aliases).

What does "NXDOMAIN" mean?

It means the domain name does not exist in DNS. Check the spelling, or the domain may not be registered.

Why do I see different results than my computer shows?

This tool queries Google’s public resolver, while your device may use your ISP’s resolver with different caching. Results can differ for a short time after DNS changes.

Is there a limit on lookups?

Google’s DNS-over-HTTPS endpoint is free and generously rate-limited. Normal manual use will not hit any limit.
