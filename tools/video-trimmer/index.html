Trim Video

Cut the perfect segment out of any video. Pick your start and end points, then export just that clip — either as a lightning-fast keyframe cut or a slower frame-exact re-encode.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Processing happens 100% in your browser — your files are never uploaded. The engine (~25MB) downloads once. Large files may be slow on phones.

Cuts on keyframes — for frame-exact cuts use re-encode (slower).

How to use

  1. Choose a video file — it loads into the preview player so you can find your moment.
  2. Click "Load video engine" — a one-time ~25MB download that enables trimming.
  3. Set the start and end times with the sliders or by typing seconds.
  4. Pick fast cut or frame-exact re-encode, press "Trim video", then preview and download the clip.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Trimming happens entirely on your device using a video engine that runs in your browser. Your file is never sent to a server.

Why does the fast cut start slightly before my chosen time?

Fast cuts snap to keyframes, which are reference points the encoder places every few seconds. If you need the cut at an exact frame, enable re-encode mode — it is slower but frame-exact.

What is the difference between fast cut and re-encode?

Fast cut copies the video stream without re-compressing it, so it finishes in seconds with no quality loss. Re-encode re-compresses the clip, which takes longer but cuts at the exact frame you picked.

Why does the engine need to download ~25MB first?

Video processing needs a full video codec, which downloads once and is then cached by your browser. After that, trimming runs fully offline.

Will trimming reduce the video quality?

Not in fast-cut mode — the video stream is copied untouched. Re-encode mode re-compresses with high-quality settings, so any loss is minimal.
