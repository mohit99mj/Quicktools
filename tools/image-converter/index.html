Frequently asked questions

What happens to transparency when converting to JPG?

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas become white (the canvas background) when you convert a PNG or WebP to JPG.

Which format should I choose?

JPG for photos and maximum compatibility, PNG when you need transparency or pixel-perfect graphics, WebP for the best size-to-quality ratio in modern browsers.

Does converting change the image size in pixels?

No. Conversion keeps the original dimensions; only the file format (and quality for JPG/WebP) changes. Images over 4000 px on the longest side are capped at that size.

Is there a limit on how many images I can convert?

No limit — convert one image at a time, as many times as you like, all free.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion happens with a canvas in your browser, so your files never leave your device.
