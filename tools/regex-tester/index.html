Regex Tester

Test JavaScript regular expressions against your text with live results. See every match with its position and capture groups, a total match count, and a live preview of find-and-replace. Input is capped at 50,000 characters and 200 matches to keep the page responsive.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

Flags

How to use

  1. Type your regular expression in the pattern field.
  2. Tick the flags you need (g, i, m, s, u, y).
  3. Paste your test string — matches highlight live with indexes and groups.
  4. Optionally enter a replacement and preview the replace result.

Frequently asked questions

Which regex flavor does this tester use?

JavaScript (the same engine as your browser). Patterns you validate here work directly in JS code, and flags g, i, m, s, u, y are supported.

How do capture groups work?

Wrap part of the pattern in parentheses, e.g. (\d+)-(\d+). Each match lists its groups in order, so you can see exactly what each group captured.

What replacement patterns can I use?

Standard JavaScript replacement tokens: $1, $2 for capture groups, $& for the whole match, $$ for a literal dollar sign.

Why is my text limited to 50,000 characters?

Very long inputs combined with complex patterns can freeze a page (catastrophic backtracking). The cap keeps the tester fast and responsive.

What does the "s" flag do?

The s (dotAll) flag makes . match newline characters too, so a pattern like a.+b can span multiple lines.
