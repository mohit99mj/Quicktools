Age Calculator

Find out your exact age from your date of birth — in years, months and days, not just a rounded number. The calculator also shows your total days, weeks and months alive, and counts down to your next birthday.

It handles leap years and month lengths correctly, so 29 February birthdays and edge cases like month-end dates come out right. Everything is computed in your browser; no data is stored or sent anywhere.

Defaults to today. Set any date after the date of birth.

Your inputs never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Enter your date of birth using the date picker.
  2. Pick the "as of" date (defaults to today) to calculate age at any point in time.
  3. Press Calculate age to see the full breakdown and your next birthday countdown.

Frequently asked questions

How is age in years, months and days calculated?

Full years are counted first, then the remaining full months, then the leftover days — borrowing days from the previous month when needed, which is why month lengths and leap years matter.

What happens for a 29 February birthday in a non-leap year?

The next birthday is shown on 28 February, the common convention used by most age calculators.

Can I calculate someone's age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the "as of" date to any date after the date of birth to see the age on that day.

Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser and your dates never leave your device.
