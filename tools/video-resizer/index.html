Resize Video

Make a video smaller — in pixels, not just megabytes. This free video resizer changes the resolution of any video to 720p, 480p, 360p or a custom width, with the height set automatically to preserve the aspect ratio.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Processing happens 100% in your browser — your files are never uploaded. The engine (~25MB) downloads once. Large files may be slow on phones.

The height is set automatically to preserve the aspect ratio.

Width must be even — odd values are rounded down.

Download resized video

How to use

  1. Choose a video file from your device.
  2. Click "Load video engine" — a one-time ~25MB download that enables resizing.
  3. Pick a preset (720p, 480p, 360p) or enter a custom width, then press "Resize video".
  4. Preview the resized video and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Resizing happens entirely on your device using a video engine that runs in your browser. Your file is never sent to a server.

Does resizing keep the aspect ratio?

Yes. You choose the width and the height is calculated automatically so the video is never stretched or squashed.

Should I upscale a small video to 720p?

You can, but upscaling does not add detail — the picture will just be larger and softer. Resizing is most useful for downscaling large videos to share-friendly sizes.

Why does the engine need to download ~25MB first?

Video processing needs a full video codec, which downloads once and is then cached by your browser. After that, resizing runs fully offline.

Does the audio change?

No. The audio stream is copied untouched — only the video resolution is re-encoded.
