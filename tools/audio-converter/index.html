Audio Converter

Convert audio files between MP3, WAV and OGG without installing anything. Pick a file from your device, choose the output format, and get a converted file in seconds — all inside your browser.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Processing happens 100% in your browser — your files are never uploaded. The engine (~25MB) downloads once. Large files may be slow on phones.

Download converted audio

How to use

  1. Choose an audio file (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A and more) from your device.
  2. Click "Load audio engine" — a one-time ~25MB download that enables conversion.
  3. Pick the output format — MP3, WAV or OGG — and press "Convert audio".
  4. Play the preview and download your converted file.

Frequently asked questions

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion happens entirely on your device using an audio engine that runs in your browser. Your file is never sent to a server.

Which output format should I choose?

MP3 is the most compatible choice for phones and players. WAV is uncompressed and best for editing. OGG is a high-quality open format that works well on the web.

What conversion settings are used?

MP3 is encoded at 192k bitrate, WAV uses uncompressed 16-bit PCM, and OGG uses quality level 4 — sensible defaults for everyday use.

Will converting lose quality?

Converting to a lossy format like MP3 or OGG discards some detail, especially if the source was already compressed. Converting to WAV does not add back quality that was already lost.

Why does the engine need to download ~25MB first?

Audio conversion needs a full media codec, which downloads once and is then cached by your browser. After that, conversion runs fully offline.
