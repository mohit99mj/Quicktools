Tip Calculator

No more mental math at the table. Enter the bill, pick a tip percentage and this free tip calculator instantly shows the tip amount, the total bill and what each person pays when you split it.

Quick-select buttons cover the usual 10%, 15% and 20%, or type any custom percentage. Everything runs in your browser — no data stored, no sign-up.

How to use

  1. Enter the bill amount before tip.
  2. Choose a tip percentage (or type a custom one).
  3. Set the number of people splitting the bill.
  4. Press Calculate tip to see the tip, total and per-person share.

Frequently asked questions

What is a standard tip percentage?

In the US, 15–20% is standard for sit-down restaurants, with 20% common for great service. This calculator accepts any percentage you prefer.

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Custom varies, but tipping on the pre-tax subtotal is the common convention. Either way, enter the bill figure you want the tip based on.

How is the per-person amount calculated?

The total bill (bill + tip) is divided evenly by the number of people. If shares are uneven, use the total and split manually.

Can I use a custom tip like 18%?

Yes — type any percentage into the tip field instead of using the quick-select buttons.
