Mortgage Calculator

Your real monthly housing cost is more than principal and interest. This free mortgage calculator combines P&I with estimated property tax and home insurance so you see the full monthly payment before you fall in love with a listing.

Enter the home price, down payment, rate and term, plus yearly tax and insurance figures, to get an instant estimate. All math happens in your browser — nothing is stored or shared.

Estimate only — excludes HOA fees and mortgage insurance.

Your inputs never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Enter the home price and your down payment percentage.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate and loan term in years.
  3. Add yearly property tax and home insurance estimates.
  4. Press Calculate to see the monthly breakdown and total interest.

Frequently asked questions

What does PITI mean?

PITI stands for Principal, Interest, Taxes and Insurance — the four parts of a typical monthly mortgage payment. This calculator shows each part separately.

How does the down payment affect my monthly payment?

A bigger down payment means a smaller loan, which lowers both the monthly P&I and the total interest paid. It can also remove the need for private mortgage insurance.

Are property tax and insurance exact?

No — they are estimates you provide, since both vary by location and property. Enter your best yearly figures for a realistic total.

Does this include HOA fees or PMI?

No. Add HOA dues or private mortgage insurance to the monthly total yourself if they apply to you.
