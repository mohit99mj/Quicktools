Image Resizer

Resize any photo to exact pixel dimensions or scale it by a percentage — ideal for profile pictures, thumbnails, and images that need to fit a size limit.

Keep "Lock aspect ratio" on to resize proportionally with no stretching. Like every QuickTools utility, it runs entirely in your browser and your files never leave your device.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

Drop an image here or browse

JPG, PNG or WebP. Output is capped at 4000 px on the longest side.

How to use

  1. Upload an image (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  2. Choose pixels or percentage mode and enter your target size.
  3. Keep "Lock aspect ratio" checked to avoid distortion, or uncheck it for a custom stretch.
  4. Click "Resize & download" to save the resized image.

Frequently asked questions

Will resizing make my image blurry?

Downsizing keeps images sharp. Upscaling beyond the original size can look soft because no new detail is created — the tool warns you when you go above 100%.

What does "lock aspect ratio" do?

It keeps the width-to-height proportions fixed, so the image scales without stretching. Change one side and the other updates automatically.

What is the maximum output size?

4000 px on the longest side. Larger inputs are capped so processing stays fast on phones and laptops.

Which format will the resized image be?

You choose JPEG, PNG or WebP before downloading. JPEG is the default for photos.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. Resizing happens in your browser with a canvas, and your files never leave your device.
