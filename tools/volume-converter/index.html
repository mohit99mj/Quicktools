Volume Converter

Convert volume between metric units (milliliters, liters, cubic meters) and US customary kitchen units (teaspoons, tablespoons, fluid ounces, cups, pints, quarts, gallons). Handy for recipes, fuel and lab work — results update live.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

US customary units: cup = 236.588 ml, fl oz = 29.5735 ml, gal = 3785.41 ml.

How to use

  1. Type the volume value you want to convert.
  2. Choose the unit to convert from and the unit to convert to.
  3. Read the live result and the exact conversion formula below it.
  4. Use the swap button to flip the conversion direction.

Frequently asked questions

How many liters are in a gallon?

One US gallon equals 3.78541 liters. (The UK imperial gallon is larger at 4.54609 liters — this converter uses US gallons.)

How many cups are in a liter?

One liter equals 4.22675 US cups. One US cup is 236.588 ml.

How many tablespoons are in a cup?

There are 16 US tablespoons in one US cup, and 3 teaspoons in one tablespoon.

Are these US or imperial units?

US customary units: the teaspoon (4.92892 ml), tablespoon, fluid ounce, cup, pint, quart and gallon used here are the US definitions common in American recipes.
