PDF Editor

PDF Editor lets you fix up a PDF's pages without installing anything: delete unwanted pages, move pages into a new order with the arrow buttons, and rotate sideways pages upright.

Work on a visual thumbnail grid, then click Save PDF to download a brand-new file with your changes applied. Your original file is never modified.

Drag & drop your PDF here

One PDF file at a time.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Drop your PDF into the upload area, or click Browse files to select it.
  2. Use the thumbnails: delete pages you don't need, move pages left or right, rotate in 90° steps.
  3. Review the final page order in the grid.
  4. Click Save PDF to download the edited document.

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit the text inside the PDF?

No. This editor works at the page level — it deletes, reorders and rotates whole pages. It cannot change text, images or other content on a page.

Is the rotation permanent in the saved file?

Yes. Rotations are written into the new PDF, so the pages open correctly rotated in any PDF reader.

Does editing change my original file?

No. Your original file stays untouched on your device; you download a separate new PDF with your changes.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your files never leave your device — all editing runs locally in your browser.
