Merge PDF

Combine several PDFs into one clean document. Merge PDF joins your files in the exact order you choose — ideal for assembling reports, job applications, invoices or scanned paperwork.

Add your PDFs, arrange them with the up and down buttons, remove any you don't need, then merge and download a single file. Pages inside each file keep their original order.

Drag & drop PDF files here

Add two or more PDF files. They merge in the listed order.

    Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

    How to use

    1. Drop your PDF files into the upload area, or click Browse files to select several at once.
    2. Arrange the merge order with the Up and Down buttons; remove files you don't need.
    3. Click Merge PDFs — files are joined top to bottom in the listed order.
    4. Save the downloaded merged.pdf file.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    There is no fixed limit, but very large files or dozens of documents can be slow on low-memory devices. For most everyday jobs, merging a handful of PDFs works smoothly.

    Can I change the order of the files?

    Yes. Use the Up and Down buttons next to each file to set the merge order. Pages within each individual file always keep their original sequence.

    Are bookmarks, links or form fields preserved?

    Only page content is merged. Interactive elements such as form fields, annotations and bookmarks may not carry over into the combined file.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Your files never leave your device — the entire merge runs locally in your browser.
