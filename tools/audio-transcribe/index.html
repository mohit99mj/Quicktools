Audio to Text

Turn speech into text in seconds. Audio to Text transcribes your audio and video files with timestamps — perfect for video scripts, captions, meeting notes and interviews.

Drop in an MP3, WAV or MP4, click Transcribe, then copy or download your timestamped transcript. The speech model runs entirely on your device — your files never leave your browser.

How to use

  1. Drop an audio or video file into the upload area, or click Browse files to choose one from your device.
  2. Click Transcribe — the first run downloads a ~40MB speech model once (you will see live progress); it is cached afterwards.
  3. Watch each segment transcribe with live progress, then review the timestamped transcript.
  4. Copy the text or download it as a .txt file for your scripts and captions.

Frequently asked questions

How does Audio to Text transcribe my files?

It runs a speech-recognition model (Whisper) directly in your browser. Your audio is decoded, split into short segments and converted to text with timestamps — your files never leave your device.

Which file formats can I transcribe?

Most common audio formats (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG) and video files (MP4, WebM) work — the audio track is extracted from videos automatically. If a file will not decode, convert it to MP3 or WAV and try again.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Transcription runs 100% on your device. The only thing downloaded is the speech model (~40MB, one time, cached afterwards) — your audio and transcript never leave your browser.

Why is the first transcription slower?

The first time you click Transcribe, the tool downloads the speech model (about 40MB). It is cached in your browser afterwards, so later transcriptions start immediately. You will see live download progress during the one-time fetch.

How accurate is the transcription?

The built-in model is fast and lightweight, so it handles clear speech well but can struggle with heavy accents, background noise, music, or several people talking at once. Always review the transcript before publishing scripts or captions.

How long can my audio or video be?

Best results come from files under about 10 minutes. Longer files need more memory and can crash mobile browsers — files over 20 minutes are refused, and very long files may fail with an out-of-memory message. Split long recordings into shorter clips for best results.

Can I use it offline?

Not fully — the page and the speech model load from the internet. But once the model is cached, repeat transcriptions do not re-download it, and your files are processed locally either way.
