Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate X percent of Y?

Divide X by 100 and multiply by Y. For example, 20% of 150 is 0.20 x 150 = 30.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 30 is 30 / 150 x 100 = 20% of 150.

How is percent change calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100. A price rising from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase; falling from 100 to 80 is a 20% decrease.

Why do results show up to four decimal places?

Percentages often produce long decimals (for example, 1/3 = 33.3333%). Four decimals keep answers precise without clutter.
