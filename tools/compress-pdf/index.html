Compress PDF

Oversized PDFs are painful to email, upload or share. Compress PDF shrinks your file by re-rendering each page as an optimized image at the quality level you choose.

Pick a lower quality for the smallest possible file, or a higher one to keep text crisp. The tool shows the original and compressed sizes side by side, so you can see exactly what you saved.

Drag & drop your PDF here

One PDF file at a time.

Pages are re-rendered as images — text will no longer be selectable. Best for scanned or image-based PDFs.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Drop your PDF into the upload area, or click Browse files to select it.
  2. Choose an image quality — lower means a smaller file, higher keeps pages sharper.
  3. Click Compress PDF and wait while each page is re-rendered.
  4. Compare the original and new sizes, then download the compressed file.

Frequently asked questions

How much smaller will my PDF become?

It depends on the content. Scanned or image-heavy PDFs often shrink dramatically, while PDFs that are mostly vector graphics or text shrink less. The tool always shows you the before and after sizes.

Will the text in my PDF stay selectable?

No. Pages are re-rendered as images, so text will no longer be selectable or searchable. This tool is best for scanned or image-based PDFs where that does not matter.

Which quality setting should I choose?

The balanced setting is a good starting point for most documents. Go lower if file size matters most, or higher if small text needs to stay crisp.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your files never leave your device — compression runs entirely in your browser.
