Frequently asked questions

What is the formula to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32: F = C × 9/5 + 32. So 20 °C = 68 °F.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9: C = (F − 32) × 5/9. So 98.6 °F = 37 °C.

What is absolute zero in Celsius and Fahrenheit?

Absolute zero is 0 K, which equals −273.15 °C and −459.67 °F. Nothing can be colder.

When should I use Kelvin?

Kelvin is used in science and engineering because it starts at absolute zero and has no negative values, which simplifies physics formulas.
