Frequently asked questions

What is a color harmony?

A harmony is a set of colors that work well together, derived from positions on the color wheel — complementary (opposite), analogous (neighbors) and triadic (evenly spaced in thirds).

What does the contrast ratio mean?

The WCAG contrast ratio measures how readable text in one color is on another, from 1:1 (no contrast) to 21:1 (black on white). A ratio of at least 4.5:1 is the common target for body text.

How do I copy a color code?

Click the copy button next to any HEX, RGB or HSL readout. You can also click a harmony swatch to load that color into the picker.

What is the difference between HEX, RGB and HSL?

HEX and RGB describe the same color as red, green and blue amounts. HSL describes it as hue, saturation and lightness, which is often easier when adjusting a color by hand.
