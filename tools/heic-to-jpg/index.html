HEIC to JPG

Convert HEIC to JPG right here in your browser. iPhones save photos in the efficient HEIC format, which many apps and sites still cannot open — this tool turns your .heic file into a widely compatible JPG at 90% quality.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no watermark.

Converts the first image in the file. JPG output at 90% quality.

How to use

  1. Choose a .heic file from your device (usually from an iPhone).
  2. Conversion starts automatically — this may take a few seconds for large photos.
  3. Preview the JPG, then click Download to save it.

Frequently asked questions

Does this convert HEIC to JPG on my device?

Yes. The conversion happens entirely in your browser using the heic2any library. Your photo is never uploaded to any server.

Can I convert multiple HEIC files at once?

The tool converts one file at a time. For another photo, just choose the next file — conversion restarts automatically.

Will my photo lose quality?

The JPG is encoded at 90% quality, which looks the same as the original for normal viewing. Some metadata may be dropped.

My file has several images in it. Which one gets converted?

Only the first image in the file is converted — bursts and multi-image HEIC files yield their first frame.

Why does my browser need to download a library first?

HEIC decoding needs special code that browsers do not include natively. A small converter library loads once from a CDN, then everything runs locally.
