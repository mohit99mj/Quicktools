Base64 Encoder

Encode any text to Base64 or decode Base64 back to text. The encoder is fully UTF-8 safe, so emoji and non-Latin scripts round-trip correctly — no more broken characters from naive encoders.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  1. Paste or type your text in the input box.
  2. Click Encode to get Base64, or paste Base64 and click Decode.
  3. Use Swap to flip the input and output for quick round-trips.
  4. Copy the result with one click.

Frequently asked questions

What is Base64 encoding used for?

Base64 represents binary data as plain ASCII text, commonly used for embedding images in HTML/CSS, encoding credentials in Authorization headers, and passing binary data through JSON or URLs.

Why do some Base64 strings end with = signs?

The = characters are padding. Base64 works in 3-byte groups, so inputs whose length is not a multiple of 3 get one or two = characters to complete the final 4-character block.

Is Base64 encryption?

No. Base64 is just an encoding — anyone can decode it instantly. Never use it to hide passwords or secrets.

Does it handle emoji and non-English text?

Yes. The encoder converts text to UTF-8 bytes first, so emoji, Hindi, Chinese and any Unicode text encode and decode correctly.
