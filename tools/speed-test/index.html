How to use

  1. Close heavy downloads or streams for a cleaner result.
  2. Click "Start test".
  3. Wait about 10 seconds for the ping and download burst to finish — watch the gauge climb.
  4. Compare runs: repeat the test to spot variability on your connection.

Frequently asked questions

What does this speed test measure?

Ping (median round-trip latency over 4 samples), jitter (variation between samples), and download throughput measured over a single ~7-second burst from a real CDN file.

Why is there no upload speed test?

Upload speed requires a server to receive the data, and this site has no backend — everything runs in your browser. The test honestly reports download and latency only.

Why is my download speed lower than my plan advertises?

Browser-based tests add overhead from encryption, the JavaScript runtime, and shared network paths. It is an estimate of real-world CDN throughput, not a lab measurement. Wi-Fi and other devices on the network also affect it.

Should I trust a single result?

Run the test two or three times and compare. Speed varies with network congestion, time of day, and what else is using your connection.

Does the test use my data allowance?

Yes, a little: the download phase runs a single ~7-second burst (up to ~15 MB).
