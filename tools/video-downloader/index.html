Video & Audio Downloader

Paste a video link — YouTube, a direct .mp4/.mp3 file, and more — and download it as video or extract the audio as MP3. Free, no sign-up, no watermark.

How to use

  1. Paste the video link into the box above.
  2. Choose Best video for MP4 video or Audio MP3 for sound only.
  3. Tap Download and watch the live status — the tool tries several free download services one by one until one responds.
  4. When the file is ready it saves straight to your device. If your browser opens it in a new tab instead, long-press the video and choose Save.

Frequently asked questions

Is this video downloader really free?

Yes — no sign-up, no watermark, no limits from our side. The actual downloading is done by free third-party services; we just connect you to them from your browser.

Which sites are supported?

YouTube links (watch, youtu.be, Shorts, embed) work best because we try several fallback services. Direct file links ending in .mp4, .mp3, .webm or .m4a always work. Instagram, TikTok, X and Facebook links sometimes work through one of the services, but support is unreliable.

Why did I get "all download services are busy"?

The services this tool relies on are free and run by volunteers, so they go up and down. If every service fails, wait a few minutes and try again — a different service may be online by then.

Do you store my links or the videos?

No. We run no servers of our own. Your link is sent to third-party download services to resolve it, and nothing is stored by us.

Is it legal to download videos?

Only download videos you own, have permission for, or that are explicitly free to download (for example Creative Commons). Respect copyright law and each site's terms of service.

Why did the video open in a new tab instead of downloading?

Some file hosts block direct saving from other websites (a browser security rule called CORS). When that happens we open the file in a new tab instead — long-press the video (or right-click on desktop) and choose Save to keep it.

Why can't it download private, login-walled or age-restricted videos?

The free services can't see videos that need a login or age check, and YouTube actively blocks download tools, so those videos fail. Public videos work best.
