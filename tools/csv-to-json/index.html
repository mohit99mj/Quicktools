CSV to JSON

Convert CSV to JSON instantly: paste your spreadsheet data or upload a .csv file. The delimiter (comma, semicolon, or tab) is detected automatically, and quoted fields — including commas, escaped quotes, and line breaks inside quotes — are parsed correctly.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser. Preview the first 20 rows, then copy or download the result.

How to use

  1. Paste CSV text below, or upload a .csv file.
  2. Click "Convert" — the delimiter is detected automatically.
  3. Check the preview table (first 20 rows) for correctness.
  4. Copy the JSON to your clipboard or download it as a .json file.

Frequently asked questions

Which delimiters are supported?

Comma, semicolon and tab are detected automatically from your data, so European-style semicolon CSVs work without any setting.

Does it handle quotes and line breaks inside cells?

Yes. Standard CSV quoting is supported: quoted fields may contain commas, an escaped pair of quotes becomes one quote character, and line breaks inside quotes stay in the same cell.

What happens to the header row?

The first row becomes the JSON object keys. Empty headers are named column_1, column_2 and so on, and short rows get null for missing values.

How big a CSV can I convert?

There is no set limit — typical exports of tens of thousands of rows convert fine. Extremely large files may be slow since everything runs on your device.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Parsing and preview happen entirely in your browser, so your data never leaves your device.
