Compress Video

Large video files are hard to share, email or upload. This free video compressor shrinks MP4 and other video files right in your browser, with three quality levels so you control the trade-off between file size and picture quality.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and the compressed video has no watermark.

Processing happens 100% in your browser — your files are never uploaded. The engine (~25MB) downloads once. Large files may be slow on phones.

Encodes at the bitrate your target needs — usually a single pass, with one refinement pass only if the first misses ±5%. Re-encoding video is slow, especially on phones.

How to use

  1. Choose a video file from your device.
  2. Click "Load video engine" — a one-time ~25MB download that enables processing.
  3. Pick a quality level (High, Balanced or Small) and press "Compress video".
  4. Compare the before/after file sizes, preview the result and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. The video is processed entirely on your device using a video engine that runs in your browser. Your file is never sent to a server.

How much smaller will my video get?

It depends on the source. High-bitrate recordings can shrink 50–80%, while already-compressed videos shrink less. The tool shows the before/after sizes so you can see exactly what you saved.

Which quality setting should I choose?

High keeps near-original quality with moderate savings. Balanced is a good default for sharing online. Small gives the smallest file but with visibly lower quality.

Why does the engine need to download ~25MB first?

Video compression needs a full video codec, which downloads once and is then cached by your browser. After that, compression runs fully offline.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes, but large videos can be slow on phones with limited memory. For best results on mobile, use shorter clips or the "Small" quality setting.
