How to use

  1. Upload a photo using the dropzone below (JPG, PNG or WebP).

  3. Preview the cut-out on a checkerboard transparency background.
  4. Click "Download PNG" to save the transparent result.

Frequently asked questions

Is this background remover really free?

Yes. The AI model runs on your own device, so there are no servers, accounts or watermarks. Use it as often as you like.

Why does it download a ~40MB model first?

The AI needs its neural-network weights to separate foreground from background. They download once and are reused for every later photo, so subsequent removals start instantly.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Processing happens entirely in your browser with on-device AI. Your files never leave your device.

What image formats can I upload?

JPG, PNG, WebP and most other browser-readable formats. The result always downloads as a PNG with transparency.

Why did the result keep part of the background?

AI segmentation works best with clear contrast between subject and background. Very busy or low-contrast backgrounds can leave artifacts — try a photo with a simpler background.
