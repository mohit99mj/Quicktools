Frequently asked questions

How do I convert km/h to mph?

Multiply km/h by 0.621371. So 100 km/h = 62.1371 mph. Select km/h → mph in the converter to do it instantly.

How fast is 1 knot in km/h?

One knot equals exactly 1.852 km/h (one nautical mile per hour). It is the standard speed unit in aviation and maritime navigation.

What is the speed of sound in km/h?

About 1,235 km/h at sea level (343 m/s), though it varies with temperature and altitude.
