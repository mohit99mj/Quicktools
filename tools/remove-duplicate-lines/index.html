Remove Duplicate Lines

A remove-duplicate-lines tool that deletes repeated lines from any list while keeping the first occurrence in its original place — your order never changes. Paste emails, URLs, keywords or any line-based list.

Fine-tune the matching with case-insensitive comparison, whitespace trimming and empty-line removal. The tool reports exactly how many duplicates were removed.

Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser.

Options

How to use

  1. Paste your list into the input box, one item per line.
  2. Set the matching options: ignore case, trim whitespace, remove empty lines.
  3. The deduplicated list appears instantly, with a count of removed duplicates.
  4. Copy the cleaned list with one click.

Frequently asked questions

Does it keep the original order?

Yes. The first occurrence of each line is kept in place and later repeats are removed, so your list order never changes.

What does "ignore case" do?

With it on, lines that differ only in capitalization — for example "Apple" and "apple" — count as duplicates, and only the first is kept.

What counts as an empty line?

A line with no characters. With "trim whitespace" on, lines containing only spaces or tabs count as empty too.

Is there a limit to how many lines I can paste?

No hard limit — it runs entirely in your browser, though extremely large pastes (hundreds of thousands of lines) may take a moment to process.
